# Affiliate money + multi-format adverts

## Who pays / who is paid

| Role | Pays | Receives |
|------|------|----------|
| **Business** (merchant offer) | The commission % (or flat fee) they set on each attributed sale | Sale revenue minus commission |
| **Promoter** (Basic or anyone with a hop link) | Nothing for the sale | Commission when a sale is attributed to their hop |

Attribution: hop click → cookie → sale reported via **Report sale** or merchant **postback**.

## Dashboards

- **Promoter:** Affiliates → Promoting (clicks, conversions, hop link) + Promoter earnings (products sold via links, request payout)
- **Business:** Seller programs + Business sales & payouts (volume, commissions owed) + Adverts & expiry (paid / sponsored / featured / promoted / banner / affiliate)

## Repost one advert into many formats

From **Adverts & expiry** → Repost formats. Choose free, paid, featured, sponsored, promoted, banner, and/or affiliate. Each selected format opens a create form prefilled with the same title/description so you can increase reach.
